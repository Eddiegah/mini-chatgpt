# Generation Samples

This file will be updated with real examples at different training checkpoints.
Run `python src/train.py` first, then `python src/generate.py` to populate this.

## Format

Each entry records: epoch, temperature, top_k, prompt, and the model's output.
Comparing early vs. late training shows the model moving from near-random text
toward Shakespearean style — one of the most satisfying aspects of training from scratch.
